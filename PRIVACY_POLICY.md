Display Control+ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-03-21

This Privacy Policy explains how Display Control+ handles information.

1. Data Collection
Display Control+ is primarily a desktop application, but paid billing and account restore features use a hosted licensing service.

2. Local Data Storage
The app may store local files such as:
- configuration settings;
- log files;
- process lock/state files;
- local license/account state such as billing email, recovery code, and entitlement cache.
These files are used to provide app functionality and diagnostics.

3. Personal Data
If you start a paid checkout, restore an account, or manage billing, the app and hosted licensing service may process your billing email address, recovery code, subscription status, and purchase metadata needed to provide paid access.

4. Network Activity
Display Control+ does not require ongoing cloud connectivity for core overlay features. However, the app may contact hosted services for:
- checkout initiation;
- purchase confirmation;
- account restore and entitlement sync;
- billing portal access;
- update checks.

5. Third-Party Dependencies
The app may use third-party services and libraries, including Stripe for payment processing and related billing infrastructure. Their behavior is governed by their respective terms and privacy practices.

6. Data Security
You are responsible for securing your own device and user account. Knight Logics recommends standard endpoint protections and regular system updates.

7. Changes to this Policy
Knight Logics may update this policy from time to time. The effective date will reflect the latest revision.

8. Contact
For privacy questions, contact Knight Logics through the official website.
